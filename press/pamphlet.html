Two moths,
one light

Hand a friend the second controller. Your trails can join: a loop made from both moths' light catches everything inside it, its fireflies are worth double, and it leaves a power-up for whoever is quickest.

A team loop, closing

When a moth is caught it becomes a pale spirit. Fly into it and it's back in the air, safe for two seconds.

Fly a loop of light

Every loop is a lantern

1

Fly. Your moth leaves a trail of glowing dots.

2

Loop. Close your trail around a creature and it wraps into a cocoon.

3

Collect. Catch the firefly that hatches before it wakes up again.

Loop a ledge to catch what walks on it

Free every firefly and the whole cave lights up. Time you save carries into the next cave.

Six creatures

Dust bunnyWalks its ledge

Shadow batFlees your light, then dozes

Snuffer beetleEats your trail

Mimic stoneWakes when the light finds it

WispSteals your light

Moth-eater spiderSpins webs that slow you

Each one pauses at some point. Learn when, and loop it then.

Three bosses

The Great OwlLoop its moon orb while feathers fall.

The Cave ToadLoop its bubbles. Mind the tongue.

The Snuffer QueenLoop her embers while her larvae eat your trail.

Every fifth cave holds a boss. You can't loop a boss itself: loop the glowing thing it sends out, and it takes the hit.
